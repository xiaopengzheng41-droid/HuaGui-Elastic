Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how Shantou Huagui Industrial Co., Ltd. ("Huagui", "we", "us") handles personal information submitted through huaguielastic.com or sent to our business contact channels.

Information We Collect

When you send a business inquiry, we may collect your name, company, country or region, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, business role, product interests, target quantity, trade preferences, message, and any files or product references you choose to provide. We also record the inquiry source page and limited attribution details such as the first landing path, referring website host, and UTM campaign parameters.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to quotation, sample, product-development, distributor, wholesale, importer, or agency inquiries.
  • To prepare product recommendations, specifications, quotations, samples, and order communication.
  • To protect the inquiry form from abuse and maintain the security of our website.
  • To keep reasonable business records relating to an inquiry or commercial relationship.

Sharing And International Communication

Huagui does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the website, store inquiries, deliver internal new-inquiry email notifications, host business email, or support international sales communication. Because Huagui operates from China and serves global buyers, your inquiry may be transferred to and handled in China.

Retention And Security

We retain inquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain business records, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. We use reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect inquiry information, but no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information associated with your inquiry, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements. You may also ask us to stop non-essential follow-up communication.

Cookies And Local Storage

The public website uses the page URL to keep the selected language. It may store your analytics choice and limited session attribution details so an inquiry can be connected to its first landing page and campaign. If Google Analytics is configured, it loads only after you accept analytics. We use it to measure anonymous page visits and inquiry actions, and we do not send names, companies, email addresses, phone numbers, or inquiry messages to Google Analytics. You can change your choice at any time through the Analytics preferences control in the footer. We do not currently use advertising cookies or Meta Pixel. Essential administrative authentication is restricted to authorized website administrators.
